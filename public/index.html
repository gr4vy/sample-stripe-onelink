Gr4vy + Stripe Onelink

Two ways to render Stripe Onelink directly on your own page (integration_client: web), both against your Gr4vy account.

1 — Full Stripe Payment Element →

Mounts Stripe's Payment Element: the card form plus the inline Onelink/Link "use your saved information" prompt for returning buyers — the whole Stripe web UI.

2 — Onelink button only →

Mounts only the Express Checkout Element — the green Onelink/Link button on its own, no card form.
